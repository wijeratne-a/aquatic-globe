# DRAFT — Commercial use request to the AquaMaps team

**To:** Rainer Froese (rfroese@geomar.de), Kristin Kaschner
**Subject:** Request for commercial-use permission for AquaMaps range maps
**Status:** DRAFT — needs Akil's approval before sending

---

Dear Dr. Froese and Dr. Kaschner,

I'm building a public website that puts AquaMaps species range maps inside a modern, interactive 3D globe — think Google Earth for aquatic species ranges, one page per species, aimed at students, divers, anglers, and the generally curious. The goal is to bring your work to a much wider audience than the current AquaMaps site reaches.

I note that AquaMaps content is licensed CC-BY-NC 3.0. The site will be free to use; I plan to support it with advertising/sponsorship, which I understand counts as commercial use — hence this email.

I'm writing to ask:

1. Whether you'd grant permission for commercial use of the AquaMaps range maps (with full attribution and backlinks to aquamaps.org on every species page, plus the Kaschner et al. citation), and
2. Whether there are terms you'd want (e.g., a license fee, revenue share, usage limits, or review of how the maps are presented).

A few specifics on how the data would be used:

- Maps shown as illustrative range heatmaps, always labeled "illustrative range (AquaMaps model)" with your disclaimer that many maps are not expert-verified.
- Served from our own tile rendering of the `aquamapsdata` datasets — no scraping of aquamaps.org, no load on your servers.
- Prominent attribution: "Range data: AquaMaps (Kaschner et al.), aquamaps.org" with a backlink on every species page.

If a call would be easier, I'm happy to work around your schedule.

Thank you for making AquaMaps openly available — it's an extraordinary resource, and I'd like to help far more people see it.

Best regards,
Akil
